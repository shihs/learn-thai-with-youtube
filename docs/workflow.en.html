Thai Subtitle Pipeline

What happens between pasting a YouTube video and seeing romanization, translation and word cards for every line: the steps, the services they call, and what each step saves under data/.

What each step does

  1. Register the job

    No new job starts if the same video is already running, or if its analysis is complete and the 「重抓字幕」 (refetch) switch is off. Otherwise the job runs in the background and the frontend keeps asking for progress.

    backend/jobs.py · start_job

  2. Get the subtitles

    YouTube is asked first for manually uploaded Thai captions. If there are none, the video URL goes to ElevenLabs Scribe v2 with diarization on and SRT as an extra output format. The whole raw response is saved.

    backend/youtube.py · backend/elevenlabs_api.py

  3. Clean up the ElevenLabs output

    The SRT has a space between every Thai character. Those are removed, and spaces between English words are kept. At speaker changes some lines come back with the same start and end time, so they would never appear on screen. The speaker of each character decides which line they belong to (see the next section).

    generate_transcript_json · merge_zero_length_segments

  4. Analyze each line with GPT

    Lines are cut into chunks of 10 and up to 5 chunks are sent to gpt-4.1-mini at a time. Each line comes back with its romanization, a Chinese translation, and the type and meaning of every word in that line. Lines whose Thai text and format version are unchanged come from the cache. Every finished chunk is saved, so a failed run resumes where it stopped.

    backend/gpt_teacher.py · analyze_long_text

  5. Check, and resend what fails

    Two conditions. The words must join back into the original line. Any word that is not a name may tokenize into at most 4 PyThaiNLP tokens, otherwise a phrase was returned as one word. A failing line is resent once on its own. If it still fails, the first result is kept and the line is never paid for again.

    backend/analysis_check.py · backend/segmenter.py

  6. Look up words, merge, save

    Words that appear in the analysis but not in the dictionary are looked up 40 at a time for romanization, parts, common meanings and usage. A romanization that is not in RTGS form is looked up once more, and any tone marks or hyphens still left are stripped. Finally each line is merged with the dictionary and written to the subtitle file the frontend reads.

    backend/dictionary.py · backend/rtgs.py

How zero-length lines are placed

Of the 911 lines ElevenLabs returned, 13 have the same start and end time, and all of them sit at a speaker change. Comparing the speaker with the lines before and after separates three cases.

8 of the 13 are interjections and 5 are fragments. When a line is merged into the next one but the next one starts later, it is shown on its own until then, for at most 2 seconds.

What the 「重抓字幕」 (refetch) switch changes

Off (default)

  • Reuses the subtitles saved in transcripts/
  • Only analyzes lines that have no analysis yet
  • Returns "subtitles already exist" when the analysis is complete
  • Does not pick up changes to the clean-up rules

On

  • Asks YouTube for uploaded captions again
  • Reads the saved ElevenLabs response and cleans it up again
  • Sends lines whose number or text changed to GPT again
  • Leaves the old subtitle file untouched if it fails
